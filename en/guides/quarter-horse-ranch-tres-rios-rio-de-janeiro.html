A Quarter Horse ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro

B2B Ranch breeds and trains Quarter Horses in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro state, among lakes and hills, with a focus on barrel racing.

Where we are

B2B Ranch is in Três Rios, in the state of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The ranch has a lake, paddocks and pastures, where the horses are born, raised and prepared.

What we do

  • Breeding Quarter Horses with selected genetics.
  • Training athletes for barrel racing and pole bending.
  • Reproduction with embryo transfer and artificial insemination.
  • The B2B Ranch & Friends Auction, with more than 17 editions.

Competing

Horses from the program compete in the RJQM State Championship, with rounds at Rancho Gabriela in Campos dos Goytacazes, and also in the Heringer Ranch Championship. B2B Ranch and Rancho 3D also offer an incentive program at the state championship.
