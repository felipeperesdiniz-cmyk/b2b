The Potro do Futuro: the event that reveals new generations

The competition for young horses that reveals the promise of each Quarter Horse generation in Brazil, similar to a futurity in the United States.

What it is

The Potro do Futuro, Portuguese for “foal of the future”, is a competition for young horses of the same generation, equivalent to futurities in the United States. The title appears at national events, such as the ABQM one, and at rounds of regional championships.

Why it matters

For breeders, it is the first big measure of a generation. A good result adds value to the horse, its parents and the breeding program.

  • B2B Lovergirl Rolls: champion of the ABQM Potro do Futuro.
  • B2B Brave Rolls: Top 3 in the Open category at the 2026 Potro do Futuro Final.
  • B2B Bulla Guy: second in the Potro do Futuro Final at the 7th Heringer Ranch Championship, in 17.103.
