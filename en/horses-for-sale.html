Horses for sale

Quarter Horses and embryos for sale at B2B Ranch

How to buy horses and embryos from the B2B Ranch program: auctions, direct offers and talking to the team.

Availability

Horses and embryos available change through the year, and the team confirms them directly, on WhatsApp at +55 (24) 99944-2266 or by email at b2branchagencia@gmail.com.

Auctions

The B2B Ranch & Friends Auction, with more than 17 editions, takes place during the ABQM Congress. The ranch also takes part in partners' auctions, as it has with Rancho 3D and the 4th 3DM Auction.

Embryos

B2B Ranch has offered embryos from mares in the band, such as Mended Heart and MJP Epic Sis. Ask the team about current offers.

Bloodlines in the program

The program brings together the stallions Tux N Rolls and B2B Lucius Ta Fame, the genetics of Adios Pantalones, and imported broodmares. See also the genetics and breeding page.

Buyers from other countries

B2B The Fling French was bought at a B2B Ranch auction, exported, and earned over US$8,000 in Houston. The terms of each case are discussed with the team.
