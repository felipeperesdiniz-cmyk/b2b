History

Lucius is by Tux N Rolls out of KR The Black Pearl. He is a full brother to B2B Maximus Rolls and a paternal half brother to B2B Lovergirl Rolls, the only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing.

He is as great a stallion in breeding as he was in the arena. He is the sire of B2B Brave Rolls, Top 3 at the 2026 ABQM Potro do Futuro final, with R$ 50,958.00 in SGP earnings and running in the 16s, and of B2B Aphrodite Rolls, one of the first generations to prove itself in competition, with a best time of 16.671.
