Tux N Rolls

First Down Dash × Zevis Doll
The stallion

The foundation of B2B Ranch. A son of the legendary First Down Dash, Tux N Rolls was one of the leading sires of the breed in Brazil and left barrel racing champions in every generation of the program.

History

With the blood of First Down Dash and a speed index of 97, Tux N Rolls became the backbone of B2B Ranch breeding.

His offspring have earned 7,672 ABQM points and R$ 5,018,420.75 on the SGP record, and Tux N Rolls is enrolled in the Seleção Nacional incentive. They include the Triple Crown winner B2B Lovergirl Rolls and the stallion B2B Lucius Ta Fame, who carries the line forward.

Tux N Rolls died in May 2023, at the age of 24.
