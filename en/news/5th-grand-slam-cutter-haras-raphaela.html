B2B Ranch wins at the 5th Grand Slam Cutter, Haras Raphaela

From November 4 to 9, 2025 at Haras Raphaela, B2B Ranch won the Tira-Teima with B2B Quaker Tux Guy and the Silver Final with B2B Aphrodite Rolls.

  • 1st · Tira-Teima: B2B Quaker Tux Guy · 16.584
  • 7th · Tira-Teima: Mended Heart · 16.672
  • 1st · Potro do Futuro Silver Final: B2B Aphrodite Rolls · 16.915
  • 7th · Potro do Futuro Qualifier: B2B Apollo Rolls · 17.069

B2B Quaker Tux Guy is by Tux N Rolls out of B2B Movidas Guy. Mended Heart is by Heart Of The Cartel out of French Bar Belle. B2B Aphrodite Rolls is by B2B Lucius Ta Fame out of Three Carat Diamond. B2B Apollo Rolls is by Tux N Rolls out of Custom Made Kat.
