Thanks and news from the B2B Ranch breeding program

HP Boom Shockalocka returns to the team and B2B Ranch invests in new embryos, in partnership with Ycambi Ranch and Rancho 3D.

We congratulate the 3DM Family on the auction held at their home, and Rancho 3D and Haras Zopone on buying the embryo from our Mended Heart by Tres Seis.

News from May 25, 2025

  • Purchase of an embryo from BR Ladybugsmoonshine by The Goodbye Lane, offered by Ycambi Ranch.
  • Buying back HP Boom Shockalocka, sold at the 15th Auction during the 2023 ABQM Congress. She returns to the team and stays at Haras 3DM.
  • In partnership with Rancho 3D, investment in a new embryo: Miss Victory Fly LW by The Goodbye Lane.

We thank all the partners, breeders and buyers who share our passion for genetic improvement.
