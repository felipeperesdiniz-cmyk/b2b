B2B Ranch at round 4 of the 25th Western Regional Championship

From May 28 to 31, 2025 at Haras Raphaela in Tietê, São Paulo, Mended Heart and Keverson de Souza placed second in Open Senior.

  • 2nd · Open Senior: Mended Heart, Keverson de Souza · 16.660
  • 10th · Beginner Horse: B2B Aphrodite Rolls, Keverson de Souza · 17.199

B2B Aphrodite Rolls, a 2025 Potro do Futuro filly, is by B2B Lucius Ta Fame out of the broodmare Three Carat Diamond, and represents the new generation of B2B Ranch breeding.

Congratulations to our athletes, trainer and the whole team on their performance and dedication.
