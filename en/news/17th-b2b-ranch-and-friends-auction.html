17th B2B Ranch & Friends Auction in Araçatuba

Held on April 25, 2025 in Araçatuba, São Paulo, during the ABQM Congress, with the participation of leading breeders.

On April 25, 2025 in Araçatuba, São Paulo, the 17th B2B Ranch & Friends Auction brought together high-performance horses during the ABQM Congress, with the special participation of leading breeders.

Pioneering in Brazilian barrel racing

We were the first to bring a stallion by the legendary First Down Dash to the sport in Brazil. We brought Epic Leader to Brazil, we have the only full sister to A Smooth Guy in the country, and we keep a direct line from the legendary King Ranch, through El Shady Zorrero and Failas Ambassador.

  • We bred the only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing, B2B Lovergirl Rolls.
  • We bred the 2018 Horse of the Year, B2B Maximus Rolls.
  • Tux N Rolls is Top 11 in all-time earnings.
