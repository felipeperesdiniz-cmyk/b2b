B2B Ranch results at round 1 of the 41st RJQM Championship

From August 13 to 16 at Rancho Gabriela, B2B Ranch earned results in barrel racing and in pole bending.

This round, held at Rancho Gabriela, reinforced ongoing work built on careful genetic selection, technical horse management, specialized training and the daily dedication of the whole team.

Barrel racing

Barrel racing demands precision, speed and complete understanding between horse and rider. Every hundredth of a second is decisive.

  • 1st · Open Senior: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson · 16.556
  • 3rd · Open Gelding: B2B Runnerway Rolls, Claudio Fria · 17.346
  • 1st · Potro do Futuro: B2B Brave Rolls, Keverson · 17.014
  • 2nd · Potro do Futuro: B2B Bullet Fool, Keverson · 17.367
  • 5th · Potro do Futuro: B2B Bulla Guy, Keverson · 17.480
  • 5th · Light Professional: Doquinho Rolls, João Gabriel · 17.392
  • 3rd · Derby: B2B Anitta French, Keverson · 17.713
  • 1st · ABQM GP: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson · 17.048

In Open Senior, B2B Quaker Tux Guy set the fastest time of the event in the category.

Pole bending

Pole bending demands agility, control, precision and explosive speed. It is a technical event in which consistency and the responsiveness of horse and rider are decisive.

  • 2nd · ABQM GP, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo · 20.181
  • 1st · Master, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo · 20.531
  • 2nd · Master, Pole Bending: B2B Oprah Rolls, Odilon Diniz · 21.052
  • 2nd · Amateur, Pole Bending: B2B Sexylover Rolls, Ryan Pereira · 20.721
  • 1st · Light Amateur, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo · 20.198
  • 3rd · Light Amateur, Pole Bending: B2B Sexylover Rolls, Ryan Pereira · 20.831

Genetics, training and performance

Genetic selection is the starting point. Training turns potential into results. And performance in the arena confirms a consistent, technical process. B2B Ranch thanks all the athletes, riders, trainers, owners and professionals involved in this round.
