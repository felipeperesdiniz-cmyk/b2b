Barretos: the stage where great stories are written

Three riders brought the strength of Tux N Rolls bloodlines to the Barretos arena, and reached the podium.

Barretos is not just an arena. It is where the big names meet, where every second counts, and where genetics, talent and preparation are put to the test.

  • Maiara Oliveira, with JJ King Tux B2B
  • Ana Laura Savini, with B2B Noa Carat Rolls
  • Yasmin de Matteo, with B2B Madeleine Rolls

Three riders, three great athletes, three different stories, united by bloodlines that have already proven their worth in competition. At Barretos, alongside their owners, they were among the leading names and reached the podium, taking the Tux N Rolls name even higher.

Results do not happen by chance. They come from genetics, selection, training, partnership and trust. And when the same bloodlines keep appearing among the big results, they stop being a promise and become proven production.

Tux N Rolls

A stallion of unquestionable genetics and a producer of great athletes. At Barretos his offspring showed exactly that: the speed to compete, the talent to win and the bloodlines to make history.
