B2B Ranch at the 46th Potro do Futuro 2025, Champions Cup and Derby

Horses from the B2B Ranch breeding program stood out at the 46th Quarter Horse Potro do Futuro, the 18th Champions Cup and the 11th Derby.

  • 1st · Open Gelding, Pole Bending, Potro do Futuro: B2B Against The Wind, Leonardo Coelho
  • 1st · Open, Pole Bending, Potro do Futuro: Camomila Killer, Keverson de Souza
  • 2nd · Light Professional, Pole Bending, Potro do Futuro: Camomila Killer, Keverson de Souza
  • 3rd · Open, Pole Bending, Derby: Totus Tux Ta Fame, Edmárcio Donã
  • 1st · Youth 14 to 18, Derby: Totus Tux Ta Fame, Luiza Bovo
  • 1st · ABQM GP, Champions Cup: Totus Tux Ta Fame, Luiza Bovo

B2B Against The Wind is by Tux N Rolls out of Guapa Fortunes OFV. Camomila Killer descends from Killer Tux B2B, a son of Tux N Rolls, and Totus Tux Ta Fame descends from Jocker Tux B2B, also a son of Tux N Rolls.

B2B Apollo Rolls, B2B Aphrodite Rolls, B2B Anitta French and B2B Alaska French also represented B2B Ranch breeding at the event.
