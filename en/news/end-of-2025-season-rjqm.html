End of the 2025 RJQM season highlights B2B Ranch breeding

Sons, daughters and grandchildren of B2B Ranch stallions confirmed high-level performance at round 3 of the 40th RJQM State Championship.

The 2025 RJQM season ended with strong results for B2B Ranch breeding, across different categories and disciplines.

Barrel racing

  • 3rd · ABQM GP 2D: B2B Poseidon Rolls, Keverson · 17.105
  • 2nd · Derby: B2B The French Girl, Keverson · 17.420
  • 2nd · Potro do Futuro 2D: B2B Aphrodite Rolls, Keverson · 17.579
  • 3rd · Open Gelding: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson · 17.201
  • 3rd · Open Junior: B2B The French Girl, Keverson · 17.064
  • 3rd · Open Senior: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson · 16.857
  • 1st · B2B Ranch Tira-Teima: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson · 16.619
  • 3rd · B2B Ranch Tira-Teima: Mended Heart, Keverson · 16.768
  • 3rd · Amateur Master: B2B Mr Postman Guy, João Paulo · 17.815

Pole bending

  • 1st · Open Junior, Pole Bending: Camomila Killer, Keverson · 20.522
  • 1st · Open Gelding, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo · 20.490
  • 1st · Potro do Futuro, Pole Bending: Camomila Killer, Keverson · 20.476
  • 2nd · Amateur Master, Pole Bending: B2B Oprah Rolls, Odilon · 22.574
  • 3rd · Amateur Master, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo
  • 1st · Amateur, Pole Bending: Buchanans Tux Wars, Carlos Eduardo · 20.881
  • 1st · Amateur Master B, Pole Bending: B2B Oprah Rolls, Odilon · 21.431

We closed 2025 proud of these achievements and renewed our commitment to excellence for 2026, always believing in the potential of the great horses we breed.
