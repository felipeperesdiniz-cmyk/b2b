B2B Ranch and Rancho 3D incentive program at the RJQM Championship

An extra R$ 5,000 prize in each of the Potro do Futuro and Beginner Horse categories for champions bred by B2B Ranch or Rancho 3D, from 2025/2026 to 2029/2030.

B2B Ranch and Rancho 3D offer an extra prize at the RJQM State Championship, in barrel racing, for the 2025/2026 to 2029/2030 seasons.

Categories and prizes

  • R$ 5,000.00 for the champion of the Potro do Futuro category
  • R$ 5,000.00 for the champion of the Beginner Horse (Juvenile) category

Who is eligible

  • Only horses bred by B2B Ranch and Rancho 3D (Rh3D), officially registered with the brand on the pedigree.
  • The horse must be champion of the RJQM Championship, counting only official results published by the RJQM.
  • It may be ridden by any competitor, as long as the breeding criteria are met.
  • Employees of B2B Ranch or Rancho 3D, and horses still owned by these ranches, are not eligible. The program is exclusively for customers who bought foals or horses from B2B Ranch or Rancho 3D.

Tie-breakers

  • Fastest individual time recorded in the championship
  • Most round wins
  • Best performance in the last round
  • A draw, if the tie remains

Prize delivery

Prizes are delivered at the end of the championship, at the official RJQM awards ceremony. The winner must present photo ID and the official registration of the horse. The prize is personal and non-transferable, and the organizers may adjust the rules with prior notice to participants.
