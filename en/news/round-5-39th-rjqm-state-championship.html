B2B Ranch at round 5 of the 39th RJQM State Championship

From May 8 to 11, 2025 at Rancho Gabriela in Campos dos Goytacazes, Rio de Janeiro, B2B-bred horses were a constant presence on the podium.

RJQM is the Quarter Horse Breeders Association of the State of Rio de Janeiro. With strong performances across several categories, horses with B2B papers reinforced the genetic and technical quality of the program.

Barrel racing

  • 1st · Beginner Horse: B2B Aphrodite Rolls, Keverson de Souza · 17.555
  • 2nd · Beginner Horse: B2B Apollo Rolls, Keverson de Souza · 17.643
  • 3rd · Beginner Horse: B2B Anakin Rolls, Felipe Zão · 17.750
  • 1st · Potro do Futuro: B2B Three Blackpearl, Keverson de Souza · 17.237
  • 3rd · Potro do Futuro: B2B Triumph Epic, Keverson de Souza · 17.465
  • 1st · Open Senior: B2B Quaker Tux Guy, Keverson de Souza · 16.841
  • 2nd · Open Senior: Mended Heart, Keverson de Souza · 16.906

Pole bending

  • 1st · Amateur Master, Pole Bending: B2B Oprah Rolls, Odilon Diniz · 21.016
  • 2nd · Open Junior, Pole Bending: B2B Apollo Rolls, Keverson de Souza · 21.252
  • 2nd · Beginner Horse, Pole Bending: B2B Apollo Rolls, Keverson de Souza · 22.711
  • 3rd · Amateur Beginner, Pole Bending: Johnny Boy Tux B2B, Bruno de Carvalho · 21.801
  • 3rd · Amateur Master B, Pole Bending: Houston Tux B2B, Odilon Diniz · 22.275

These results confirm the commitment of B2B Ranch to genetic quality, technical training and performance in the arena.
