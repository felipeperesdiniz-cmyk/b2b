Training

Training Quarter Horses for barrel racing

At B2B Ranch, the team follows each horse from the choice of the cross to training in the arena.

From breeding to the arena

B2B Ranch athletes are born, raised and prepared at the ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, with individual care for each horse. Genetic selection is the starting point, and training turns potential into results.

Disciplines

The program focuses on barrel racing, and horses from the program also compete in pole bending.

Results in the arena

  • B2B Quaker Tux Guy, with Keverson de Souza: won the ABQM GP at round 1 of the 2026 Copa Organnact in 16.489.
  • B2B Brave Rolls, with Keverson de Souza: Top 3 in the Open category at the 2026 Potro do Futuro Final.
  • Mended Heart, with Keverson de Souza: second in Open Senior in 16.659 at the 3rd Rancho Gabriela Grand Prix.
