HP Stoli My Jet

Blazin Jetolena × Im Chica Stoli

About

Maternal half sister to Steal Money, winner of more than US$710,000 in barrel racing. Co-owned by B2B Ranch and Haras WV.

HP Stoli My Jet is a broodmare at B2B Ranch, a Quarter Horse breeding and training ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, focused on barrel racing. Sire: Blazin Jetolena. Dam: Im Chica Stoli.
