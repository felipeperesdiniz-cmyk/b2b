About

Full sister to B2B Lovergirl Rolls, the only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing.

B2B Secretlover Tux is a broodmare at B2B Ranch, a Quarter Horse breeding and training ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, focused on barrel racing. Sire: Tux N Rolls. Dam: Magnolia Shady FF.
