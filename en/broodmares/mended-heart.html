Mended Heart

Heart Of The Cartel × French Bar Belle

About

Imported, the only daughter of Heart Of The Cartel in Brazil and a maternal half sister to Adios Pantalones. Best time of 16.401 s.

Ridden by Keverson de Souza, she placed second in Open Senior at the 3rd Rancho Gabriela Grand Prix (16.659) and at round 4 of the 25th Western Regional Championship (16.660).

Mended Heart is a broodmare at B2B Ranch, a Quarter Horse breeding and training ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, focused on barrel racing. Sire: Heart Of The Cartel. Dam: French Bar Belle.
