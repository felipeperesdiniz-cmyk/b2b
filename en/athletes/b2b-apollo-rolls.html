About

By Tux N Rolls out of Custom Made Kat, competitive in both barrel racing and pole bending.

B2B Apollo Rolls is an athlete bred by B2B Ranch, a Quarter Horse breeding and training ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, focused on barrel racing. Sire: Tux N Rolls. Dam: Custom Made Kat.
