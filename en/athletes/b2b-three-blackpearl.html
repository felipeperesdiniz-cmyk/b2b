About

By Tres Seis out of KR The Black Pearl. Won the Potro do Futuro Silver Final at the 3rd Rancho Gabriela Grand Prix in 17.184.

B2B Three Blackpearl is an athlete bred by B2B Ranch, a Quarter Horse breeding and training ranch in Três Rios, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, focused on barrel racing. Sire: Tres Seis. Dam: KR The Black Pearl.
