Champions and standouts from the program

B2B Lovergirl Rolls

Tux N Rolls × Magnolia Shady FF

The only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing. Triple Crown in 2017, champion of the ABQM Brazilian Congress in both amateur and open, of the ABQM Potro do Futuro, the ABQM National and the Haras Raphaela Grand Prix.

B2B Noa Carat Rolls

Tux N Rolls × Three Carat Diamond

Champion of the Barretos International Rodeo in 2023 and runner-up in 2024. Also champion of the Grand Slam Cutter, the ABQM National and the Haras Raphaela Grand Prix.

B2B Madeleine Rolls

Tux N Rolls × Magnolia Shady FF

Full sister to B2B Lovergirl Rolls. Reached the podium at Barretos with Yasmin de Matteo and won in the Amateur category in 16.549.

B2B Maximus Rolls

Tux N Rolls × KR The Black Pearl

Horse of the Year in 2018 and full brother to the stallion B2B Lucius Ta Fame.

B2B Aphrodite Rolls

B2B Lucius Ta Fame × Three Carat Diamond

One of the first generations by B2B Lucius Ta Fame to prove itself in competition, with a best time of 16.671 s.

B2B Apollo Rolls

Tux N Rolls × Custom Made Kat

By Tux N Rolls out of Custom Made Kat, competitive in both barrel racing and pole bending.

B2B Three Blackpearl

Tres Seis × KR The Black Pearl

By Tres Seis out of KR The Black Pearl. Won the Potro do Futuro Silver Final at the 3rd Rancho Gabriela Grand Prix in 17.184.

B2B The Fling French

Frenchmans Guy × Fling N Delight

Exported after being bought at the 15th B2B Ranch & Friends Auction, she earned over US$8,000 at the Houston Qualifiers & 1D Winners.

B2B Alaska French

Tux N Rolls × B2B Movidas Guy

By Tux N Rolls out of B2B Movidas Guy, full sister to B2B Quaker Tux Guy. Ran the Potro do Futuro in 2025.
