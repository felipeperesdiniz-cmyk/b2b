The Ranch

A ranch among lakes and mountains. This is where the B2B Ranch Quarter Horse athletes are born, raised and prepared, with selected genetics and individual care for each horse.

01 · Our story

Odilon and Bebel Diniz

It all began with a dream: to breed and train high-performance horses, with passion and respect for the animals.

In Três Rios, in the mountain region of Rio de Janeiro state, that means more than 26 years in the equestrian world, with first-class facilities and a team that follows each horse from the choice of the cross to training in the arena.

02 · Values

What guides us

  • ExcellenceThe highest standard in genetics, horse care and training.
  • CommitmentTransparency and dedication to every partner.
  • Respect for the animalsCare, ethics and individual attention for every horse.
  • PassionWe love what we do, and it shows in every detail.
03 · Genetics

Careful selection

Every horse goes through a selection focused on improving the breed and on athletic performance.

We invest in advanced reproductive technology, such as embryo transfer and artificial insemination, to get the most from our results.

Broodmares

The mares behind every generation at B2B Ranch: imported, from rare bloodlines, and some still competing.Swipe to see them all →

  1. B2B Movidas GuyFrenchmans Guy × Docs Little Movida

    The only broodmare in Brazil who is a full sister to A Smooth Guy, a stallion with more than US$5 million as a sire. Dam of B2B Quaker Tux Guy.

  2. B2B Ophelia RollsTux N Rolls × Glisten Failas KRB

    King Ranch bloodline, through the strength of Failas Ambassador. ABQM point earner; dam of B2B Anitta French.

  3. B2B Ornella RollsTux N Rolls × Three Carat Diamond

    Sister to B2B Noa Carat Rolls, the top rodeo money earner in Brazil. A broodmare chosen to produce athletes.

  4. B2B Reality ZorreroAlso an athleteEl Shady Zorrero × Julie Tux Failas B2B

    A gray mare with a best time of 16.688 s and several titles. Now also a major project as a broodmare.

  5. B2B Secretlover TuxTux N Rolls × Magnolia Shady FF

    Full sister to B2B Lovergirl Rolls, the only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing.

  6. Magnolia Shady FFShady Leo × Bifriend Lady FF

    Dam of the only Triple Crown winner in Brazil, B2B Lovergirl Rolls, and of B2B Madeleine Rolls; her offspring have earned more than R$ 621,000 in prizes.

  7. Frenchmans BullaFrenchmans Guy × Bulla Dash

    Dam of B2B Morgana Rolls, considered her leading daughter. Co-owned by B2B Ranch and Haras Zopone.

  8. B2B Morgana RollsAlso an athleteTux N Rolls × Frenchmans Bulla

    Considered the leading daughter of Frenchmans Bulla. She runs in the 16s and is now also an embryo donor, with a foal already running in the 16s.

  9. Julie Tux Failas B2BTux N Rolls × Glisten Failas KRB

    Dam of B2B Reality Zorrero, by El Shady Zorrero. A name of great importance in the history of the Quarter Horse in Brazil.

  10. HP Boom ShockalockaSlick By Design × Boom Goes The Fame

    Imported. Full sister to Rollin In The Fame and Rockette Ta Fame; dam of B2B Theodora Rolls.

  11. Mended HeartAlso an athleteHeart Of The Cartel × French Bar Belle

    Imported, the only daughter of Heart Of The Cartel in Brazil and a maternal half sister to Adios Pantalones. Best time of 16.401 s.

  12. Lenas Bar BelleAlso an athleteBlazin Jetolena × French Bar Belle

    Papers unique in Brazil: by Blazin Jetolena out of French Bar Belle, by Frenchmans Guy, and a maternal half sister to Adios Pantalones.

  13. Custom Made KatCustom Crome × Princessontheprowl

    By Custom Crome out of Princessontheprowl, with Princess In Diamonds, one of the great reining broodmares, as her second dam. Princess In Diamonds, by Shining Spark, became one of the most important broodmares in the discipline, with offspring exceeding US$1 million in earnings.

  14. Guapa Fortunes OFVEl Shady Zorrero × Miss Fortunes Fool

    Daughter of the legendary Miss Fortunes Fool, by Dash Ta Fame. Dam of B2B Tapdancer Rolls and B2B Bullet Fool.

  15. MJP Epic SisAlso an athleteEpic Leader × Royal Sissy Irish

    A direct daughter of Epic Leader, papers never before seen in Brazil, from the family of four-time world champion DM Sissy Hayday.

  16. Simply EpicEpic Leader × A Girl Needs Cash

    Imported. Dam of B2B Simply Rolls, B2B Triumph Rolls and B2B Star Famous Epic.

  17. Three Carat DiamondTres Seis × Dangle A Carat

    Imported. Dam of B2B Noa Carat Rolls, two-time Barretos champion and champion of the Brazilian Congress, the ABQM National and the Grand Prix.

  18. HP Stoli My JetAlso an athleteBlazin Jetolena × Im Chica Stoli

    Maternal half sister to Steal Money, winner of more than US$710,000 in barrel racing. Co-owned by B2B Ranch and Haras WV.
