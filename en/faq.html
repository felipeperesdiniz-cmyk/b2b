FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers about B2B Ranch, its horses and how to get in touch.

What is B2B Ranch?
It is a ranch that breeds and trains high-performance Quarter Horses for barrel racing, founded by Odilon and Maria Izabel (Bebel) Diniz.
Where is B2B Ranch?
In Três Rios, in the state of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
Who are the B2B Ranch stallions?
Tux N Rolls, the foundation of the program (1999–2023), B2B Lucius Ta Fame, and Adios Pantalones, whose genetics reach Brazil through a syndicate that includes B2B Ranch.
How do I ask about breeding or buying horses?
On WhatsApp at +55 (24) 99944-2266 or by email at b2branchagencia@gmail.com. The team confirms availability directly.
Can I visit the ranch?
Contact the team on WhatsApp to arrange a visit.
Can buyers from other countries get in touch?
Yes. One horse from the program, B2B The Fling French, was exported after a B2B Ranch auction and earned over US$8,000 in Houston. The terms of each case are discussed with the team.
What is the B2B Ranch &amp; Friends Auction?
It is the ranch's auction, with more than 17 editions, held during the ABQM Congress with invited breeders.
Where can I see the horses&#x27; results?
On the news page and on each horse's page. Videos of the runs are on YouTube, and photos are on Instagram and TikTok.
Who is B2B Lovergirl Rolls?
The only Triple Crown winner in Brazilian barrel racing, by Tux N Rolls out of Magnolia Shady FF, bred by B2B Ranch.
