Genetics and breeding

Quarter Horse genetics and breeding

The stallions, broodmares and bloodlines of B2B Ranch, and how to talk to the ranch about breeding, genetics and available horses.

The stallions

  • Tux N Rolls (First Down Dash × Zevis Doll): the foundation of the program, with 7,672 ABQM points and R$ 5,018,420.75 in offspring earnings on the SGP barrel racing record.
  • B2B Lucius Ta Fame (Tux N Rolls × KR The Black Pearl): born at B2B Ranch, sire of B2B Brave Rolls and B2B Aphrodite Rolls.
  • Adios Pantalones (Tres Seis × French Bar Belle): the first and only stallion to pass US$1 million in earnings. His genetics reach Brazil through the Adios Pantalones syndicate, formed by B2B Ranch, Haras Zopone, Ycambi Ranch, Haras Fazenda São José, Fazenda Poço das Pedras and Witzler Ranch.

The broodmares

The band includes imported mares and rare bloodlines, such as Mended Heart, by Heart Of The Cartel; Three Carat Diamond, dam of B2B Noa Carat Rolls; and B2B Movidas Guy, full sister to A Smooth Guy. See the whole band on the broodmares page, and how to buy horses and embryos.
